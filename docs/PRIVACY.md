## The short version

- Lightline works on your phone first. Its Daily Guide learns your patterns **on your phone**.
- **Your bank alerts and spending never leave your phone** unless you switch on "Include money totals", and even then only totals are sent, never the alert text, names or account digits.
- The reports you choose to share with your community (light on/off, bank network problems, fuel prices and fares) are sent to our server so neighbours can see them. They are not linked to your name.
- You can delete what the Daily Guide has learned at any time ("Forget everything").

## 1. What stays on your phone

| Data | What it is used for | Leaves your phone? |
|---|---|---|
| Bank and fintech alerts you paste into Money | Sorting spending (POS, fuel, urgent 2k…), daily and weekly totals, budget warnings | **No.** Balances are removed and account numbers cut to the last 2 digits when you paste. |
| The Daily Guide's memory (your light reports, generator runs and refuels, prices you saw, your bank reports, for up to 90 days) | Learning your routines and making predictions and reminders | **No.** |
| Your Guide settings (reminder frequency, quiet hours, budget, language) | How the Guide behaves | **No.** |
| Generator details (tank size, burn rate, fuel price), chores | Fuel calculations and power-triggered chores | **No.** |

All of this is stored in the app's private storage on your device. Uninstalling the app or using "Forget everything" (Guide) and "Delete all alerts" (Money) removes it.

## 2. What is sent to our server, and why

**Community reports (when you tap them):** light status for an area ("Up NEPA", "They took light", low voltage), bank and POS network outcomes (instant, delayed, failed), fuel, gas and fare prices with location names you type, and areas you add. These are shared so that people in your area get live information. They include the area and state, the time, and what you reported. They do **not** include your name, phone number, bank alerts or account details.

**Smarter chat answers (off by default):** if you switch on "Smarter chat answers (online)", questions you ask the Daily Guide are sent to our server together with a short summary of figures the app already shows you (light pattern, generator fuel, fuel prices, public bank network status). Questions about your spending, "your bank" and the morning brief are answered on your phone and not sent, unless you also switch on "Include money totals". With that switch on, only totals are added (for example "spent today", "urgent 2k transfers this week", your weekly budget and which bank most of your alerts come from).

**Technical data:** our server sees your device's IP address when the app connects. We use it only to protect the service (for example, limiting how many chat questions one device can send in 10 minutes) and do not store it with your reports.

## 3. AI processing and transfer outside Nigeria

When smarter chat answers are on, our server sends the question and the summary described above to **Anthropic** (Claude), an AI provider based in the United States, to word the reply. The app checks every number in the reply against the figures it sent; if the AI gives a number that was not in your figures, the app shows its own on-phone answer instead.

## 7. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the Daily Guide schedules reminders on your phone. You choose how many (Off, Few: up to 3 a day, Normal: up to 5 a day) and set quiet hours. Reminders are created on your phone, not sent from our server.
